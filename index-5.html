FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

Who is online coaching for?

For anyone who wants structured, personalized training with remote follow-up from the coach.

Can beginners join?

Yes. Programs are built around your level, and the Beginner level focuses on technique, consistency and confidence.

Can I train at home?

Programs are built around your body, schedule and level. Tell the coach what you have available during your consultation.

Do you provide nutrition guidance?

Yes, general nutrition guidance linked to your training goals. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment; for health conditions, consult a doctor.

How does online coaching work?

You start with a consultation, receive a plan built around your goals and level, train on your schedule and share your progress so the coach can follow up.

How often do we check progress?

It depends on the program: Transform and Elite include weekly check-ins, and Starter includes progress tracking.

Can the program be adjusted?

Yes. Transform and Elite include program adjustments based on your progress.

How do I start?

Choose your goal and program in the Start Coaching section and request a free consultation, or message the coach on WhatsApp.
